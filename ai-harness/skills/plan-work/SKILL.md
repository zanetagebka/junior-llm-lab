# Plan work

Read [the shared contract](../../references/workflow.md). Resolve the supplied
brief or existing workspace. Read its actual requirements without progressing
beyond planning for a plan-only request.

Follow [task research](../implement-task/references/research.md) and
[task planning](../implement-task/references/plan.md). Preserve existing notes
and decisions. Deliver a concrete file/responsibility map, ordered behavior
slices, dependencies, acceptance and failure checks, and unresolved decisions.
Keep the amount of detail proportional to the change.

Record the plan and next implementation action in the private workspace.
Planning does not authorize application edits, commits, or publication.
