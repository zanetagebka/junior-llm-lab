# Task 20 — Capstone

**Module 10 — Product judgment**

**Lovable PoC:** Prototype one meaningful product improvement selected from user/eval/operational evidence. State the hypothesis before building.

**Rails/Ruby:** Implement the production-shaped vertical slice, tests and evaluation. Add/update ADR and threat considerations where relevant.

**Defence:** Demo the prototype and real implementation side by side. Explain architecture without AI, respond to a requested code change, show before/after evidence, close the private Learning Agent log, and submit a final [lesson handoff](../templates/LESSON_HANDOFF.md). Internal company data remains outside the lab until the [internal-use gate](../INTERNAL_USE_GATE.md) is met.

**Final gate:** The Junior can distinguish clearly between what the prototype proved, what the implementation proved and what still requires evidence.

## Completion

Follow the [lesson workflow](../WORKFLOW.md). Keep the Lovable change small, implement the agreed behavior in Ruby or Rails, show verification, and close the session in the private Learning Agent log.
